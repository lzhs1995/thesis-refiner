For this new package, distinguish:

1. Structural/privacy checks of the distributable files.
2. Actual loading and use by a named client.
3. Live success of a particular source/tool route.
4. Acceptance of specific observations under a research contract.
5. Formal research adoption and any separately authorized modeling.
